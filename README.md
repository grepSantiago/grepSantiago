### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Systems Engineer** and **Fullstack & AI Engineer**. My focus is on building solid, scalable software from end to end—combining clean architecture and robust engineering foundations with real-world AI integrations.

- 🚀 **Projects & Ventures**:
  - **Founder @ [BitHue](https://bithue.com)**: Helping companies establish strong digital identities and building high-performance, tailored software solutions.
  - **Creator @ [SpotAlly](https://spotally.app)**: A smart travel platform created to help people discover, curate, and explore travel spots and experiences.

- 🤖 **AI Integration & Engineering**:
  - Designing and deploying **LLM-driven features and modern AI capabilities** across web and mobile products—ranging from semantic search and intelligent normalization to contextual assistants and moderation guardrails with reliable data grounding.

- 🧱 **Architecture & Principles**:
  - Strong advocate of **SOLID principles**, **Domain-Driven Design (DDD)**, and strict type safety across the entire stack.
  - Experienced with Linux environments, containerization with Docker, and clean system design.

- 🌱 **Currently Exploring & Learning**:
  - Native iOS & iPadOS development with **Swift & SwiftUI**, developing an offline-first modular notes app.
  - Autonomous multi-agent architectures and LLM evaluation benchmarks.

- 💬 **Ask me about**: Fullstack architectures (TypeScript, Node.js, NestJS, React, Next.js, Python), database modeling with PostgreSQL & Prisma, or integrating AI models into production software.

- 📬 **Let's Connect**: Catch me on [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/santiago-guzman-dev) or drop a message via my [portfolio contact form](https://santiagoguzman.dev/#contact).
